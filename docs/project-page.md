## Summary

FAC Ultra is Caleb Robinson's fishing game: cast a line, catch fish, earn upgrades
and chase a bigger catch, right in a web browser.

## About

FAC Ultra started as Caleb's own project, and it is still his game — Robinson AI
Systems hosts it and helps keep it online. The current build runs in the browser,
so there is nothing to install: open the play address and start fishing.

## Features

- Playable in a web browser on a PC, tablet or phone — no download or install
- Twenty waters and 500 kinds of fish, from a pond to the First Water, with a boss fish to beat in each
- A live fight on the water: hold to reel, ease off when the fish runs
- Gear and upgrades: rods, reels, boats, lures and bait, and fishing line that wears out and can be crafted
- Six ways to play: Expedition (the career, with five difficulty levels and three save slots), Story (twenty guided chapters), Recreational (relaxed, kept apart from your career), Survival, Local Co-op and Tournaments
- An aquarium that earns while you are away, contracts and rebirth
- Rare events after long play that turn some fish into named mutations
- A guided tutorial that teaches new players on the water, read aloud
- Promo codes: type a code in Settings and claim its reward
- Night fishing once five bosses are beaten and level 45 is reached — fish keep biting while you are away
- Prestige at level 100, which opens the final water
- Holiday events, three days each, with their own mutation and 50x luck, fish worth and XP
- Tournaments scored by the server, with live boards, friends, teams and trophies
- Optional sign-in for cloud saves and family profiles, plus save codes
- Kid Mode with a Helping Hand, five picture-quality levels, full screen and colour-blind colours
