## The three rules

1. **Your website section is yours to edit — and you can't break Dad's website.**
   Your pages have their own corner on his website, and since November 2026 you
   can redesign that whole corner yourself: layout, colours, pages, words. You
   work in your own copy of the website files (your *fork*), your account can't
   change the real website at all, and Dad's side reads every draft and does the
   publishing. The full story, in plain words, is the START-HERE file in the
   website repo's `docs/caleb` folder — your agent knows where it is.
2. **To change your page, just say what you want in normal words.** Tell your AI
   agent something like *"update my page to say the swamp level is finished"* or
   *"make my section dark blue"*. For news about the game, the agent updates the
   project files here in this repo. For changing how your section looks, the
   agent edits your corner in your copy of the website. Either way, Dad's side
   checks the result and publishes it to the real website. You don't need to
   know how the publishing part works.
3. **Only share the website addresses above.** The preview inside Grok (or any
   other builder) is your workshop — it's a test copy, links to it don't work
   for friends, and it can change or disappear. The website address is the
   storefront: it always stays the same, even if the game moves computers.

## Words you might see

- **Repository (repo)** — a project's home folder, which also remembers every
  change ever made to it. FAC Ultra's repo is this one.
- **Preview** — a private test copy of the game. Great for building, not for
  sharing.
- **Publish** — Dad's side putting your update onto the real website where
  everyone can see it.
- **Fork** — your own copy of a project's files, under your account. You can
  change anything in your copy; it becomes real only when the owner's side
  publishes from it. Your website section works this way.
- **Agent** — an AI helper (like the one that helped you build the game, or
  Dad's). Agents working on your projects follow the playbook in
  `docs/website/AGENT-PLAYBOOK.md`.

That's everything you need. Build cool stuff; the paperwork handles itself.
